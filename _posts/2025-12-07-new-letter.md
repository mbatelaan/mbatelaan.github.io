Last month a letter I wrote with Jo Dudek and Robert Edwards was published in Physics Review Letters under the title ["η and η′ Production in J/ψ Radiative Decays from Quantum Chromodynamics"](https://doi.org/10.1103/tdk2-bk8w). This covers the research I worked on during my time at the College of William & Mary and Jefferson Lab in the US.

In this letter we show the results of the first calculation of the radiative decays $J/\psi \to \gamma \eta$ from first principles.
