Last month a letter I wrote with Jo Dudek and Robert Edwards was published in Physical Review Letters under the title ["η and η′ Production in J/ψ Radiative Decays from Quantum Chromodynamics"](https://doi.org/10.1103/tdk2-bk8w). This covers the research I worked on during my time at the College of William & Mary and Jefferson Lab in the US. A more detailed description of the method is presented in the accompanying paper published in Physical Review D ["η and η′ meson production in J/ψ radiative decays from lattice QCD"](https://doi.org/10.1103/jvpg-3ph4).

In this letter we show the results of the first calculation of the radiative decays $J/\psi$ to $\gamma \eta$ and $\gamma \eta'$ from first principles. The results from the calculation can be seen in the figure below where we have shown the transition form factor as a function of momentum transfer for both radiative decays.
![Fig1](/assets/physics/FIG2__dt16_both_proj_letter.png)
To enable this calculation we developed some new technology which exploited the exact symmetries of the lattice to average over various momentum combinations.
